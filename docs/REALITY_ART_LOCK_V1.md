# PHU QUOC PIXEL - REALITY ART LOCK V1

Status: LOCKED
Scope: Welcome, PQC Arrival, Island Map, Sunset Town / No Brakes

## Core rule

Real Phu Quoc owns geography and landmark relationships. The approved gameplay key art owns mood, camera language, palette density and pixel-art finish.

Pipeline:

REAL REFERENCE -> SPATIAL LOCK -> PIXEL ADAPTATION -> GAMEPLAY -> PRODUCTION QA

Never invent a landmark position only to make the frame prettier.

## Art direction

- Portrait 9:16.
- Rich pixel-postcard look, not primitive retro blocks.
- Warm tropical sunset, turquoise sea, terracotta roofs, cream facades, bougainvillea and black street lamps.
- Dense micro-detail, readable silhouettes, limited coherent palette.
- Rear-chase camera for No Brakes. Rider sits in the lower third and faces away from camera.
- UI, rider, obstacles and world must share the same outline weight, highlight logic and pixel language.
- Approved gameplay reference is the composition north star, not a literal geography source.

## Sunset Town reality lock

Official reference: https://sungroup.com.vn/en/sunsettown

- Sunset Town is on the southwest coast of Phu Quoc.
- Central Village Clock Tower is a major sea-facing landmark in the La Festa / Central Village area.
- Official Sun Group material describes the clock tower as 75 m high.
- Kiss Bridge is an offshore waterfront landmark, not a road bridge.
- Kiss Bridge has two branches that approach each other over the sea and remain separated at the tips.
- The bridge spans about 800 m in total and the central gap is about 30 cm.
- An Thoi Cable Car Station is geographically distinct from Central Village. Cable-car cabins must only appear when a verified gameplay sightline supports them.

### No Brakes frame order

Near -> far:

1. Rider / scooter

4. Mediterranean-inspired Sunset Town facades

This ordering may be compressed for gameplay readability, but landmark relationships must not be reversed.

## PQC reality lock

References:
- https://www.vietnamairport.vn/vi/cang-hang-khong
- https://www.phuquocairport.com/phu-quoc-airport-quick-facts/

- IATA: PQC
- ICAO: VVPQ
- Runway: 10/28
- Runway length: 3,000 m
- Airport is in Duong To.
- Terminal visual cue: low modern terminal, bright white segmented roof, glass facade, apron in front, green island terrain behind.

Airport arrival art must read as Phu Quoc International Airport rather than a generic runway.

## Island map lock

- Main island silhouette follows real geographic proportions: broad northern mass, western shoulder at Ganh Dau, eastern bulge, long taper to An Thoi.
- Pins are projected from latitude / longitude.
- Southern archipelago is shown as a separate extension below An Thoi, not fused into the main island.
- Never move a destination pin merely to improve label spacing. Move labels, not geography.

## Production definition of done

A scene is not final until:

1. geography/landmark relationship is grounded,
2. pixel art reads as one coherent scene,
3. gameplay remains readable at phone size,
4. safe-zone survives tall-phone cover crop,
5. CI/build passes,
6. production SHA gate passes,
7. E2E journey passes,
8. production screenshots pass visual review.
